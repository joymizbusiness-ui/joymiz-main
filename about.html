About Joymiz

We believe everyone deserves access to powerful productivity tools — without paying a dime or giving up their privacy.

Our Mission

Joymiz was founded on a simple idea: productivity tools should be free, instant, and private. In a world where every app wants your email, your data, and your credit card, we chose a different path.

We build tools that run entirely in your browser. No servers storing your resumes, invoices, or QR codes. No accounts to manage. No upsells. Just clean, functional tools that do what they promise.

How We Build

Every Joymiz tool is built with AI assistance and designed with care. We leverage modern web technologies to create fast, reliable experiences that work on any device — from your phone to your desktop.

By keeping everything client-side, we ensure your data stays with you. It's the way the web was meant to work.

Our Values

🎯 Simplicity

Every tool does one thing well. No bloat, no complexity — just results.

🔒 Privacy

Your data never leaves your device. We don't track, store, or sell anything.

🌐 Accessibility

Works on any device, any browser, anywhere. No downloads required.

💚 Generosity

Every feature is free. No premium tiers, no hidden costs, no tricks.

The Team

Joymiz is built by a small, passionate team that believes technology should serve people — not the other way around. We're developers, designers, and dreamers committed to making the web a better place, one free tool at a time.
