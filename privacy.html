Privacy Policy

Your privacy is not a feature — it's a foundation. Here's how we protect it.

TL;DR: We don't collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything runs in your browser. We have no servers that store your information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. All Joymiz tools run entirely in your browser. Your resumes, invoices, QR codes, and any other data you create never leave your device.

We do not use cookies for tracking or analytics. The only data processed is what you choose to keep locally in your own browser.

2. How Our Tools Work

All Joymiz tools are client-side applications. This means:

  • Your data is processed entirely in your web browser
  • No information is sent to our servers during use
  • We have no database storing your content
  • When you close the tab, your data remains only in your browser's local storage (if you choose to save it)

3. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may collect standard web server logs (IP addresses, browser type, etc.) for infrastructure purposes. We do not access or use this data for tracking.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services on our tools.

4. Data Storage

Joymiz tools may use your browser's localStorage to save your preferences. This data:

  • Stays entirely on your device
  • Is never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time through your browser settings

5. Children's Privacy

Our tools are suitable for users of all ages. Since we collect no data, there are no age-related restrictions on data collection.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We will never reduce your privacy rights without your consent.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via our contact page.
